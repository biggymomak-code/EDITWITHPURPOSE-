FAQ

Quick answers

Do you edit all genres?+

I'm open across genres — send the details and we'll see if I'm the right fit.

Will you rewrite my book?+

No. I identify what could be stronger while preserving your voice and ownership of the story.

What if I don't know what kind of editing I need?+

That's completely fine — tell me what you're struggling with and we'll work it out together.

Do you write scripts too?+

Yes — features, pilots, and script consulting. If you have a story for the screen, tell me about it.
